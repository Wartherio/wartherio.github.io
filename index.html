Shorts Factory Uploader

A private tool used by a single creator for their own YouTube channels.

The tool prepares short vertical videos. Only after the channel owner approves a video does it upload it to the owner's own YouTube channel, using YouTube API Services. Every upload starts as a private video with the publish time the owner chose. At that time the video is published, either by YouTube's own scheduler or by the tool. Until then the owner can move the time or keep the video private.

The tool is not offered to other users and does not access any channel other than the owner's.
